L0: # Why do I see a Free tier error when calling eth_getLogs with a large block range?
L1: 
L2: Last updated 1 month ago
L3: 
L4: The `eth_getLogs` method applies a block range limit that depends on your plan tier. On the Free tier, this limit is small, so a request with a wider block range fails with an error referencing the Free tier restriction.
L5: Upgrading to the Pay As You Go (PAYG) plan raises this limit substantially for most major chains, but it does not remove the limit entirely. On PAYG, a request to `eth_getLogs` on most major chains succeeds if it either stays within a 10,000 block range with no cap on response size, or spans any wider block range as long as the response contains 10,000 logs or fewer (and stays under the general 150MB response size cap). A request that satisfies neither condition is rejected, even on PAYG.
L6: The Enterprise plan can raise the block range limit further on some chains, though the exact limit still varies by chain. Contact [REDACTED_EMAIL] if you need the current limit for a specific chain on your plan.
L7: ## How to upgrade
L8: 
L9:   1. Go to cite0†Billing Settings†dashboard.alchemy.com in the Alchemy Dashboard.
L10: 
L11:   2. Select the Pay As You Go plan. PAYG has no upfront commitment, and you're billed based on compute unit usage. See cite1†Alchemy Pricing†www.alchemy.com for current rates.
L12: 
L13:   3. Once the upgrade takes effect, your `eth_getLogs` requests follow the PAYG block range and response size limits described above instead of the smaller Free tier restriction.
L14: For billing or invoice questions, contact [REDACTED_EMAIL].

L0: # What is included in the Free Tier?
L1: 
L2: Last updated 7 days ago
L3: 
L4: Free Tier Compute Units (CUs) and Compute Units Per Second (CUPs)
L5: 
L6: Here's what you can expect from the free tier:
L7: 
L8:   * Monthly Compute Units Limit: Each free tier account is allocated up to 30 million Compute Units (CUs) every month. This account-wide limit ensures you have ample capacity to run your applications and explore different products that we offer!
L9:   * Compute Units Per Second (CUPs) Limit: On an application-wide basis, you can use up to 300 Compute Units Per Second (CUPs). This rate limit allows your applications to perform operations efficiently within that limit.
L10: ## Understanding Compute Unit Costs
L11: 
L12: Every method or operation within Alchemy's services consumes a certain amount of CUs and CUPs. Knowing these costs is crucial for managing your usage and staying within the free tier limits. Detailed information on the compute unit cost for each method call is readily available, helping you plan and optimize your blockchain interactions! For a comprehensive breakdown of CU and CUP costs by method, please visit: cite0†Compute Unit Costs†www.alchemy.com .
