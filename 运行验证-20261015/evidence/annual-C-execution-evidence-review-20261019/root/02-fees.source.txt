We use a maker-taker fee model to determine trading fees. Different fees apply to orders that provide liquidity (maker orders) and to orders that take liquidity (taker orders). We calculate the fees based on the current pricing tier you are in at the time of your order execution. Trading volume is calculated across all trading pairs and is expressed in USD, based on the exchange rate at the time of the trade.

When determining a user’s pricing tier, the user’s 30-day trading volume in _FX/Stablecoin_ pairs (_see the list of applicable pairs below_) receives a reduction of 80%, with an exception for the USDT/USD and USDT/EUR pairs, which receive a reduction of 90%; the calculated adjusted volume constitutes a basis for the application of reduced fees. For example, $100 of volume in a standard FX/Stablecoin pair counts as $20 in the 30-day volume calculation, whereas $100 of volume in USDT/USD or USDT/EUR counts as $10. Volumes in all other pairs have a standard weighting of 100%.

FX / Stablecoin pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, SGD/USD, USDC/EUR, USDC/USDT, USDC/USD, USDT/EUR, USDT/USD, EURC/EUR, EURC/USDC, PYUSD/USD, EURCV/EUR, EURCV/USDT, VCHF/USD, VCHF/EUR, VEUR/USD, VEUR/EUR, ZUSD/USD, GYEN/USD, RLUSD/USD, RLUSD/EUR, RLUSD/USDT, EURCV/USDC, XSGD/USD, XSGD/USDT, DAI/USD

Placing an order that is filled instantly at the market price makes you a taker and a fee between 0.03% and 0.40% applies. Placing an order which is not filled instantly and is instead posted to the order book is a maker order. If your maker order is matched by another customer’s order, a lower fee between 0.00% and 0.30% applies.

For placing an order that is immediately only partially matched, you will pay a taker fee for that portion. What remains of the order is placed on the order book. Once it’s fully matched, it becomes a maker order, and a maker fee applies for this remaining portion of the order.

We recommend checking your fee tiers prior to trading to ensure you are in the updated pricing tier.

## Basic Trading Spread

When using Basic Trading, the displayed price includes a spread added to the spot market price available on our trading platform. As a result of a guaranteed quote, the total cost of your Basic Trading transaction may be higher than if you were to place an order directly on the order book. The spread applied in Basic Trading consists of two components:

- Basic Spread (fixed):

- Stablecoins: 0.5%

- Other crypto-assets: 1.8%

- Volatility Spread (variable):

An additional spread may apply during periods of increased market volatility.This is dynamically calculated based on real-time market conditions and the specific crypto-asset being traded. The volatility spread is intended to reflect increased market risk, particularly during high volatility or price surges. In low-volatility market, this spread is reduced to zero.

- Stablecoins: 0% to 0.5%

- Other crypto-assets: 0% to 0.5%

## Crypto Withdrawals

Transactions on cryptocurrency networks are subject to variable network transaction fees. When you withdraw your cryptocurrency, Bitstamp charges you a variable crypto withdrawal fee which consists of Bitstamp's estimate of the network transaction fees within a certain time period and Bitstamp's crypto withdrawal processing fee. The exact crypto withdrawal fee is disclosed before you confirm the cryptocurrency withdrawal.

## Minimum trade amount

The minimum order size is 10 EUR/EURCV/EUROC/GBP/RLUSD/USD/USDC/USDT for EUR/EURCV/EUROC/GBP/RLUSD/USD/USDC/USDT-denominated trading pairs, 0.0002 BTC for BTC-denominated trading pairs, and 0.002 ETH for ETH-denominated trading pairs.

## Fiat Balance Threshold at Account Termination

_Not applicable to clients of Bitstamp USA, Inc._

The minimum fiat currency balance that can be returned to you upon account termination is 25 EUR/USD. Any amount below this value will not be returned.

## Inactive Account Maintenance Fee

_Applicable only to clients of Bitstamp Limited (excluding clients resident in the United States)._

Inactive account maintenance fee

5.00 EUR/USD/GBP per month

Accounts that have been inactive for at least six (6) consecutive months may be subject to an Inactive Account Maintenance Fee. Inactivity is determined based on the absence of trading activity, deposits, withdrawals, or if the client has not logged into their account during the relevant six-month period.

If there is insufficient fiat currency available in the account to pay the fee, Bitstamp may charge the equivalent value of 5.00 EUR/USD/GBP in a supported crypto-asset held in the account, using the applicable exchange rate at the time the fee is assessed.

The fee will only be deducted from the available balance in the client’s account. If the account balance is less than the monthly fee, only the remaining balance will be deducted. The fee will never result in a negative balance.

## Transaction Recovery

10% of the value of lost cryptocurrency, but in no case less than 1,000.00 USD/EUR. This will be charged in the cryptocurrency which is subject to the transaction recovery. The minimum value of transactions that Bitstamp may attempt to recover is 10,000.00 USD/EUR.

## Fee Rounding

We kindly ask our users to take note on Bitstamp's policy regarding fee calculation. Our fees on EUR/EURCV/EUROC/GBP/RLUSD/USD/USDC/USDT-denominated pairs are calculated to 5 decimal places, while fees on BTC-denominated and ETH-denominated pairs are calculated to 8 decimal places. Additional decimal places will be rounded to the nearest value.
