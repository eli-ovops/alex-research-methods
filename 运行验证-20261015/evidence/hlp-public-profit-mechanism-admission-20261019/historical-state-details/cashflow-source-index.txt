- [Account state and leverage](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/social-trading/account-state-and-leverage.md): Latest known account state: equity, cash, open notional, unrealized PnL, **account leverage** (open notional ÷ account value) and every open position with its own leverage, entry price, mark price and accrued funding. `as_of` is the snapshot time: snapshots are hourly, so this is not real time.
- [Equity history](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/social-trading/equity-history.md): Account value over time, from hourly on-chain account snapshots. Each point carries cash, open notional, unrealized PnL, account leverage and position count; `summary` adds the change over the window and its max drawdown.
- [User performance](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/social-trading/user-performance.md): Round-trip trade statistics over a rolling window: win rate, PnL, trade count, volume, average holding time and long/short ratio.
- [PnL calendar](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/social-trading/pnl-calendar.md): One row per trading day: realized PnL from fills, fees, funding, `net_pnl = realized − fees + funding`, a running total, and the day's closing equity when snapshots cover it. `totals` sums the window and counts profitable / losing days.
- [Top trades](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/social-trading/top-trades.md): Best round-trip trades with ticker, direction, leverage, PnL, notional, entry/exit price and close date. Open positions can be ranked alongside closed ones (by unrealized PnL): they come back with `status: "open"`, no `closed_at`, and an `as_of` snapshot time.
- [Funding rate history](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/funding/funding-rate-history.md): Hourly funding snapshots for a coin. Returns fundingRate, premium and time.
- [Predicted funding rates](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/funding/predicted-funding-rates.md): Latest funding snapshot per coin (predicted next funding).
- [User funding payments](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/funding/user-funding-payments.md): Hourly funding payments of a wallet, rebuilt from Hyperliquid's official node event archive. Same data as `userFunding` over `POST /info` (Live Data REST API).
- [List liquidations](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/liquidations/list-liquidations.md): Liquidation events with optional filters on coin, user, notional amount and time range.
- [Recent liquidations](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/liquidations/recent-liquidations.md): Recent liquidation events (defaults to 2h window). Same filters as `/liquidations/`.
- [Active traders (24h)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/active-traders-24h.md): Distinct addresses that traded in the last 24 hours, and the change against the 24 hours before.
- [Volume per coin for one address](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/volume-per-coin-for-one-address.md): Volume and fill count per coin for one address, sorted by volume.

- [Daily volume (10d)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/daily-volume-10d.md): Traded notional per day over the last 10 days.
- [Top traders (24h)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/top-traders-24h.md): Top traders in the last 24 hours ranked by PnL, volume or trades.
- [Fees (24h)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/fees-24h.md): Fees over the last 24 hours, split between spot and perp.
- [Fills (24h)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/fills-24h.md): Fills over the last 24 hours, and the change against the 24 hours before.
- [Trading volume (24h)](https://docs.hypedexer.com/api-reference/overview/trading-volume-24h.md): Traded notional in USD over the last 24 hours, and the change against the 24 hours before.
