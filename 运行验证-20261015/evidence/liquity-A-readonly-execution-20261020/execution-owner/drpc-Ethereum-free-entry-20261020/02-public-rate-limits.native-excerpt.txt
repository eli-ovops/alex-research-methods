L2115: We have rate limiting for public nodes (which are always free of charge), and no rate limiting for the paid tier. Because we charge users on a per-request basis, you can make as many paid requests as you want as long as you have enough CUs in your deposit.
L2116: 
L2117: For requests to free nodes, we usually have the following rules. Please note that some networks have custom rate limits applied on the free tier.
L2118:   1. The Free Tier includes 210 million Compute Units (CU) per 30-day period, resetting every 30 days from the date of registration.
L2119:   2. Under normal conditions for the free tier, we have a limit of 120,000 CUs per minute per IP. This is approximately 100 eth_call requests per second.
L2120:   3. If there is high demand for free requests in a specific region, the limit may be reduced. The minimum limit is set at 50,400 CUs per minute, which is equivalent to 40 eth_call requests.
L2121:   4. Some methods cost 0 CU (like eth_chainId), but when rate limiting, the minimum cost of every call is 10 CU, so free methods cannot be abused.
L2122:   5. The maximum timeout is set to 2 seconds to prevent potential DDoS attacks using high-latency requests.
L2123:   6. The log limit is capped at 10,000 entries.
L2124:   7. Batch requests are limited to 3 items to prevent bypassing rate limits.
L2125:   8. Filter methods are disabled by default.
