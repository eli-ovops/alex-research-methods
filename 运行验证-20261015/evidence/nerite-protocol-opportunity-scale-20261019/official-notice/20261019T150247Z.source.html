Overview

Redemptions

1 USND is always redeemable by anyone for $1.00 worth of ETH or other collateral.

Borrowing

USND is minted when users borrow against their ETH, wstETH, rETH, rsETH, weETH, ARB, COMP, or tBTC.

User Set Rates

Users choose what interest rate to pay on their loans and can update the rate at any time.

Yield Bearing

USND can earn sustainable yield from the interest rate borrowers pay, liquidations, and incentives by depositing in the Stability Pools.

Non-Fungible Vaults

CDP troves are also standardized NFTs, so borrowers can sell a loan without closing it.

Streaming

USND is a natively streamable “super-token”. Holders can stream tokens to anyone linearly over any period of time.

Governance Minimization

The Nerite Protocol is governance minimized, so the rules stay the same and there are more security guarantees.

Delegation Aggregation

ARB tokens deposited as collateral in Nerite maintain their delegation and governance power in the Arbitrum DAO.
