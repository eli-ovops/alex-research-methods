L63@P1: characteristics of assets and portfolios that

L65@P1: volatility. We achieve this by leveraging the
L66@P1: portfolio at times of low volatility and scaling
L67@P1: down at times of high volatility. Effectively,
L68@P1: the portfolio is targeting a constant level
L69@P1: of volatility, rather than a constant level of
L70@P1: notional exposure.
