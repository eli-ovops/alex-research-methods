How credits work
API credits per minute
Every endpoint has a data weight: the number of credits one request consumes. Your quota resets every minute. There are no hidden weights beyond what the endpoint's
documentation
states.
Example
/time_series
for AAPL, MSFT, and TSLA: 1 credit × 3 symbols = 3 credits.
/income_statement
for the same three symbols: 100 credits × 3 symbols = 300 credits.
WebSocket (WS) credits
Every WebSocket method has a subscription weight, charged when you subscribe to a symbol's data stream.
Example
/quotes/price
costs 1 WS credit per successfully subscribed symbol. Subscribing to AAPL, EUR/USD, USD/JPY, BTC/USD, and ETH/BTC consumes 5 WS credits.

FAQs
What currency are your prices in?
They're in US dollars.
Do you offer discounts or free trials?
We do offer 20% students and startups discounts. Free trial is available under the Basic plan.
Learn more
Besides, we never run discounts and offer free trials on upper-tier plans.
What payment methods do you offer?
We accept PayPal and all major credit cards (including Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express and UnionPay.)
Are there any contracts, hidden charges or setup fees?
No contracts, no hidden charges, and no setup fees. You can switch your plan or cancel your Twelve Data subscription at any time.
How can I switch my plan?
Upgrade or downgrade your account anytime from your Dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately on a pro-rated basis, while downgrades and cancellations become effective at the end of your current billing period.
How can I cancel my plan?
Cancel your account anytime from your Dashboard. After canceling, you'll still be able to use your plan until the end of the subscription period.
How can I switch to annual billing?
Switch to annual billing at anytime from your Dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and any unused amount will be added to your balance.
What is your refund policy?
In most cases, payments for Twelve Data subscriptions aren't refundable.
Learn more
Can I purchase only one dataset?
No, we don't offer datasets separately. Twelve Data plans include access to all our datasets.
What are the API & WebSocket credits?
Credits are the core metric used to access data on our platform. Standard request to API consumes 1 API credit, which means that you can request approximately 610 symbols per minute on our Pro 610 plan. Assuming you want to stream the data for 300 symbols, that would cost your 300 WebSocket credits. If you need some help or advice on this, contact us anytime.
Learn more
Can you provide unlimited API or WS credits?
With a custom plan, we can provide any API & WS credits limit you require. This limit has to be specified explicitly and can not be just unlimited.
