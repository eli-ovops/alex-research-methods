## High-Throughput Examples

API credits are a per-minute rate limit. Higher tiers have a lower effective price per credit/minute, which makes scaling up cheaper than running multiple small subscriptions:

- **Pro** at $229/mo — 1,597 API credits/min: effective rate of **$0.143 per credit/min**.
- **Ultra** at $999/mo — 10,946 API credits/min: effective rate of **$0.091 per credit/min** (lowest self-serve rate on Individual).
- **Venture** at $499/mo — 2,584 API credits/min: effective rate of **$0.193 per credit/min**.
- **Enterprise** at $1,099/mo — 10,946 API credits/min: effective rate of **$0.100 per credit/min**.
- **Enterprise+**: volume-based custom pricing — effective rate trends well below $0.10 per credit/min depending on commit and add-ons.

## Individual vs. Business — How to Choose

The choice is determined by **who is using the data**, not just what the data is used for:

- **Individual plans** are for individuals only — personal research, hobby projects, learning, internal analytics for your own use, or non-commercial side projects. Display and distribution are limited to internal personal use. *Subject to VAT where applicable.*
- **Business plans** are required for any company, team, fund, or organization using Twelve Data — even if the data is only used internally and never displayed externally. Business plans also unlock external display rights and, on Enterprise tiers, external distribution.

If you are unsure which plan applies, contact sales — using the wrong plan tier may violate the terms of service.

## Data Add-Ons

Pro, Ultra, Venture, Enterprise, and Enterprise+ support data add-ons for niche datasets and additional exchange coverage. Add-ons attach to the base plan and are billed alongside the subscription. Contact sales for the current add-on catalog.

Annual billing is available on every paid plan with a **17% discount** versus monthly. Annual customers also lock in their rate for the term.

Apply via [twelvedata.com/contact](https://twelvedata.com/contact).

Discounts are applied to base subscription pricing and do not stack with annual billing's 17% discount or with each other.

## API and WebSocket Credits

**API credits** are a per-minute rate limit on REST API throughput. Free and Basic plans add an 800-request/day cap on top of the per-minute limit; all other tiers remove the daily cap.

**WS credits** govern WebSocket streaming throughput. Free, Basic, and Grow include 8 **trial** WS credits, which can only be used on Twelve Data's trial symbol set — they are intended for evaluating streaming, not for production use. Pro and above include full WS credit allocations that work across all subscribed markets and symbols (500 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,500 / 5,000 / 10,000 depending on configuration).

For volume above Enterprise tier, custom WS credit packages are available via Enterprise+.

## VAT and Taxes

Individual plan prices are listed pre-tax and may be **subject to VAT** based on the customer's billing location.
