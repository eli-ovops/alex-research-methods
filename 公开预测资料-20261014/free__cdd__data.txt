Data Integrity Notice
Even Binance
has gaps.
Major exchanges have regular outages, maintenance windows, and halted contracts —
        events that leave
critical gaps in historical OHLCV
. Those gaps
        silently
generate false breakouts, skew volatility calculations, and produce
        misleading backtest results
. The free CSVs in our archive carry the raw
        exchange data as the venue reported it, gaps and all.
