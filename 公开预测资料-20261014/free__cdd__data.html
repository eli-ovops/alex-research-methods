Data Integrity Notice

Even Binance has gaps.

Major exchanges have regular outages, maintenance windows, and halted contracts — events that leave critical gaps in historical OHLCV. Those gaps silently generate false breakouts, skew volatility calculations, and produce misleading backtest results. The free CSVs in our archive carry the raw exchange data as the venue reported it, gaps and all.

The fix: Zero-Gap OHLCV Data+ ships verified gapless 1-minute OHLCV for 8 major cryptocurrencies with row-level provenance flags (raw · reconciled · filled).
